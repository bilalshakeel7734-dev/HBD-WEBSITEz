Duas For Samra ♡

May Allah bless every step of your life and fill your future with happiness, peace and success.

♡

For Happiness

May Allah always keep a smile on your face, fill your heart with peace and give you countless reasons to be happy.

✦

For Your Future

May Allah make your future bright, guide you towards what is best for you and help you achieve your dreams.

♡

For Your Life

May Allah protect you from every difficulty, surround you with good people and bless you with a beautiful life.

✦

For Your Dreams

May every good dream in your heart become a beautiful reality and may Allah give you success in everything you do.

"May Allah make your coming years even more beautiful than the years behind you."
